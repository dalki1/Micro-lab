About MicroLab

A community of builders and an agency that ships. Same people, two sides of the same mission.

Our Story

How we started

MicroLab started in 2026 as a small group of developers who were tired of building things that never left the "MVP" stage. What began as a handful of builders comparing notes turned into a community — and soon after, an agency arm doing real client work.

We're still young, but we move like a team that's been shipping for years. Every project we take on, whether internal or for a client, gets the same standard: build it right, ship it, stand behind it.

What We Do

Community & Agency
